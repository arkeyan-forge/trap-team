# Trap Team: what stable means, and what is left

> **Purpose:** the stable release bar, evidence and remaining work by area · **Status:** current (2026-11-01) · **Tags:** [TrapTeam]

## Definition

A stable release installs from the player's disc through the launcher on macOS, Windows and Linux; the story and modes play through with supported figures and real and emulated portals; saves and figures survive restarts; audio, controls and pictures work; performance is consistent at the supported default; and the remaining limitations are documented. The current candidate is experimental and off by default (STATE (office STATE)). The bar below is a release decision for this game, not a claim that the checks have passed.

## Done

- ✅ The game builds and reaches the title, opening and playable tutorial with an emulated portal; movement and a jump were observed at 30 fps without a fault in the recorded run ([boot](boot.md#past-the-place-a-skylander-prompt-2026-10-30-v), [status](status.md#status)).
- ✅ The level tour loaded and ran 53 of 56 entries for 130 seconds; the three arena crashes were traced to missing arena meshes in the redirect, not established as player-flow failures (STATE (office STATE), [status](status.md#arenas-in-the-tour-2026-10-31-v)).
- ✅ A crafted villain trap works in normal and evolved form; entering and leaving the villain works. The Toy Box shows the held villain and evolved state; the launcher can edit them (STATE (office STATE), [Toy Box](toybox.md)).
- ✅ The Toy Box has a trap seat, figure and trap sheets, and disc-sourced pictures; its scripted check exercised the screens and placement ([Toy Box](toybox.md#proof)).
- ✅ The tutorial picture has full-scene rendering, scaling and an ultrawide view tested in the recorded scenes; the movie shader cache fix produced a visible window ([status](status.md#the-picture-scale-anisotropy-ultrawide-2026-10-30-lane-j-v), [status](status.md#status)).
- ✅ Text language selection was exercised on two disc editions; voices follow the text language in the current implementation, but have not been heard in that test ([boot](boot.md#language-2026-10-31-v)).
- ✅ The title and tutorial reached 60 fps in measured runs and the tested intro had comparable duration at 30 and 60; this does not establish all gameplay at 60 ([research](research.md#frame-rate-60-2026-10-30-and-31-lane-i-trapteam)).
- ✅ The candidate is in the kits; a released launcher fetched a kit and installed the game into a fresh folder (STATE (office STATE)).

## Left

**Blocks stable** means required before removing the experimental status. **Can wait** means a documented limitation may remain for the first stable release.

### Gameplay

- 🔧 **Blocks stable — full playthrough and modes:** the tester plays the story from a fresh save through the ending, including bosses and transitions, and exercises each available mode; a recorded pass with no progression stop or fault proves it. The level redirect tour does not prove normal flow ([status](status.md#status)).
- 🔧 **Blocks stable — arenas:** the tester enters each arena through its own selection flow and completes it; dev investigates any failure. Successful runs with the required meshes loaded prove the redirect failures do not affect players ([status](status.md#arenas-in-the-tour-2026-10-31-v)).
- 🔧 **Blocks stable — slow levels:** dev repeats the two slow tour entries on a quiet machine and investigates sustained drops; a recorded playable frame rate at the default limit proves the result (STATE (office STATE)).

### Figures and portal

- 🔧 **Blocks stable — capture and persistence:** the tester evolves a captured villain (its quest) and captures a second villain into the same trap; dev compares the trap before and after. Done [V] (2026-11-02): a capture in normal play (Sheep Creep into an empty Life Trap, emulated portal), the trap taken off and put back after the game wrote it, and a restart that keeps it, with a before/after trap diff ([boot](boot.md#the-first-capture-2026-11-02-v), `tools/regress/trapteam_capture.sh`).
- 🔧 **Blocks stable — figures and saves:** the tester tours supported figure types, two-player play and save/figure changes across a restart; dev fixes failures. Recorded acceptance, play and readback without loss prove the paths. The figure tour [V] (2026-11-07, `tools/regress/coverage_all.sh`): every toy type of the accept list (169 runs, swappers paired), the 11 traps empty and the 46 villains each in a trap of its element (`ArkeyanFigureTest --make-trap`), placed while the hero plays in the tutorial: every one read by the game, no fault; whether a held villain plays with LT is checked for Gulper only.
- 🔧 **Blocks stable — real portal and villain audio:** the tester plays with a real compatible portal and trap, including capture and villain speech; dev checks portal traffic and fixes missing behavior. A successful physical run with audible, complete speech proves it; the speaker commands are still uncertain ([research](research.md#the-speaker-villain-voices)).
- 🔧 **Blocks stable — portal prompts:** dev implements the missing automatic prompt; the tester reaches figure and trap requests in play and confirms the correct Toy Box opens. A recorded request and placement prove it; F6 alone is the current route ([toybox](toybox.md#proof)).

### Platforms

- 🔧 **Blocks stable — installation and play on each platform:** the tester installs from disc and plays on real Windows and Linux machines, and repeats the install and play on macOS with the candidate kit. Successful fresh installs, launches and save readback on each prove the release path; the existing fresh install is one example, not a platform matrix (STATE (office STATE)).

### Player experience

- 🔧 **Blocks stable — controls and audio:** the tester checks all controls in normal play, including the unresolved shoulder buttons, listens to music, voices and movies, and checks supported disc languages; dev fixes defects. Recorded correct actions and audible complete sound prove them ([status](status.md#open-problems), [boot](boot.md#language-2026-10-31-v)).
- 🔧 **Blocks stable — reliability at 30 fps:** the tester runs an extended play session and dev measures frame pacing, memory and faults on a quiet machine. A sustained run without a crash, growing memory or persistent performance failure proves the default is reliable; the existing runs are shorter ([boot](boot.md#past-the-place-a-skylander-prompt-2026-10-30-v)).
- ⏸ **Can wait — 60 fps and visual polish:** dev investigates liquids above 30 and the dark figure appearance; the user supplies a console comparison if available. Matching liquid behavior and a comparison capture prove fixes. Keep 30 fps as the default until the higher rate is validated (STATE (office STATE), [research](research.md)).
- ⏸ **Can wait — independent voice language and wider display coverage:** dev adds separate voice selection and checks additional ultrawide scenes and movies; the tester listens and reviews captures. Independent text/voice playback and correct captures prove them; document the current coupled language and display limits ([boot](boot.md#language-2026-10-31-v), [status](status.md#the-picture-scale-anisotropy-ultrawide-2026-10-30-lane-j-v)).

### Quality and docs

- 🔧 **Blocks stable — known workarounds and release notes:** dev reviews the video-pool workaround, unexplained legal-screen delay, shader coverage and remaining limitations against the final playthrough, then documents the supported configuration and known issues. A clean recorded run and updated notes prove the release decision; the pool cause remains unproved ([boot](boot.md#what-broke-on-the-way-to-a-level), [status](status.md#open-problems)).
- ⏸ **Can wait — presentation gaps:** dev documents placeholder pictures and unnamed trap shapes; the tester checks the resulting screens. Screenshots and accurate notes prove the limitation is communicated ([toybox](toybox.md#placeholders-v)).
