## Why the chapter ends are open
- **The recorded capture route drifts.** `trapteam_capture.route` replayed on the frame clock passed once of two on 2026-11-02 and failed twice of two on
  2026-11-03 (`ch1a`: Gusto dropped into the river bed after the cannon at about 170 s; `ch1b`: back at the cannon at 360 s, then the river bed): stick
  input is camera-relative and the camera turns by itself, so a timing shift of a few frames sends the replay elsewhere. It stays a tool, not a gate.
- **Live steering without a map is slow.** `ch1c` played the route in short segments with a picture after each (about 35 steps to 420 s): it opened the
  first chest (RT, then mash X) and reached the turtle gate, but the cannon that launches Gusto over the stone gate took no second RT from any side tried.
- **What would close it:** a person's run through Soda Springs to the end screen and on into the Academy, recorded as presses with times (or the owner
  playing once on a build with `SKY_TOYBOX_SCRIPT_FILE` logging), then the same for chapters 2 and 3 from the hub's chapter choice. A lead from
  TrapTeamRecomp's commit messages: they read player 1's place from memory for a status line; a position readout would let a route check itself
  and correct its drift [?].
